01 / Preprint (v1.0) Transit & Open Data · Bide+ 2026-11-16 · EN · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24286027

Accuracy of open-data versus operator real-time bus arrival predictions: a paired evaluation of the Bide+ method in Biscay, Spain

Paired empirical benchmark of 555,744 real-time arrival predictions against GPS ground truth across 59 stops in Biscay (Bizkaibus). The open-data Bide+ method achieves 64% accuracy within ±2 minutes versus 39% for the official operator app, leading at 57 of 59 stops.

02 / Preprint (v1.1) Algorithms & Prediction · Bide+ 2026-11-16 · EN · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24286302

Predicting bus arrivals when the feed reports positions but not delays: a reproducible three-layer method with an open reference implementation

Formal specification and open Python reference implementation of a three-layer arrival prediction method with explicit abstention and shrunk learned travel-time medians for SIRI-VM/GTFS feeds that omit schedule deviations. Evaluated across 9.1 million predictions (92–96% within ±3 min).

01 / Preprint (v1.0) Transit & Algorithms · Bideplus (Bide+) 2026-11-22 · EN · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24442212

Multi-criteria journey planning on the passenger's device: design and validation for fifteen merged regional transit networks

Design and validation of a multi-criteria range RAPTOR planner that runs entirely in the browser on the passenger's phone, over fifteen merged transit networks in and around the Basque Country (8,242 stops, 54,896 trips). Four labels per bag find 88% of the exact restricted Pareto set, lower bounds make plan queries 2.6 times faster, earliest arrivals agree with Connection Scan, and with the production walking table it returns 96% of OpenTripPlanner's journeys within 60 s. Median plan query: 16 ms on a recent iPhone, 65 ms on a 2021 mid-range Android phone.

02 / Preprint (v1.0) Transit & Open Data · Bideplus (Bide+) 2026-11-16 · EN · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24286027

Accuracy of open-data versus operator real-time bus arrival predictions: a paired evaluation of the Bide+ method in Biscay, Spain

Paired empirical benchmark of 555,744 real-time arrival predictions against GPS ground truth across 59 stops in Biscay (Bizkaibus). Within ±2 minutes of the actual passage: 16% for the timetable, 39% for the official real time and 64% for the open-data Bide+ method; from three minutes out, Bide+ is more often within ±2 minutes at 57 of 59 stops.

03 / Preprint (v1.1) Algorithms & Prediction · Bideplus (Bide+) 2026-11-16 · EN · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24286302

Predicting bus arrivals when the feed reports positions but not delays: a reproducible three-layer method with an open reference implementation

Formal specification and open Python reference implementation of a three-layer arrival prediction method with explicit abstention and shrunk learned travel-time medians for SIRI-VM/GTFS feeds that omit schedule deviations. In 9.1 million predictions, within ±2 minutes in 92–96% of cases when the bus is under three minutes away and 67% at 10–20 minutes, against 23–25% for the published timetable.
